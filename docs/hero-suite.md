# Track Your Hero

The **Stamina bar** (`ds-stamina-bar`) tracks a hero's Stamina — current, maximum and
temporary — plus Recoveries, Catch Breath, and the Winded and Dying states. It is useful on
a [Canvas character sheet](canvas-character-sheet.md) or in a session note, and it
**writes its state back into the note** as you play: spend a Recovery and the YAML in the
block updates itself. You never have to edit those numbers by hand.

Everything about it lives on its own page: [Stamina Bar](stamina-bar.md).

Conditions on a hero or creature in a fight are managed from the
[initiative tracker](initiative-tracker.md#conditions).
